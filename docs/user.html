👤 User & Product Guide

Designed for Calm Productivity

Learn how ViDo organizes your responsibilities without endless red lists, stress, or complexity.

1. Universal Life Contexts

ViDo replaces office jargon with clean, universal terminology that works anywhere:

🏠

Personal & Household

Medical checkups, vehicle maintenance, home repairs, and grocery routines tracked with calm accountability.

🎓

Students & Learning

Semester coursework, exams, and thesis chapters broken down into measurable progress increments.

💼

Professionals & Freelancers

Client deliverables, invoices, and project milestones with exact due times and timestamped notes.

👥

Teams & Workspaces

Shared milestone roadmaps, explicit task assignees, and progress visibility without recurring status meetings.
